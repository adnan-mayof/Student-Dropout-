When you click:

Yes → You will see which courses have the highest dropout rates and how dropout students are distributed across other factors.

No → You will see which courses have no or very low dropout and how continuing students are distributed.

In other words, the filter lets you toggle between:

* **Students who dropped out**
* **Students who continued**

All charts in the dashboard update automatically when the filter is switched.

## 📘 Introduction

This project presents an **interactive Tableau dashboard** that explores undergraduate student dropout patterns. It provides a **visual, data-driven perspective** on how dropout status is distributed across student demographics, academic performance, and socio-economic information.

**Goals of the dashboard:**

* Examine how dropout relates to different factors
* Explore dropout versus non-dropout populations interactively
* Support evidence-based planning, resource allocation, and early interventions

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Student dropout is a critical challenge for universities, affecting both student outcomes and institutional performance. By understanding the **distribution of dropout vs. non-dropout students**, institutions can identify risk areas and strengthen support services.

---

## ❓ Key Question

* What differences can be observed between students who drop out and those who persist in their studies?

---

## 📊 Dataset

The data comes from the **VALORIZA Research Center and Polytechnic Institute of Portalegre**, originally shared on Kaggle. It includes:

* Demographics (e.g., age, application mode)
* Socio-economic status (scholarship, tuition fees up to date)
* Academic records (course enrollment, grades, curricular units per semester)

This dataset was designed to study **dropout and success rates** across undergraduate programs.

👉 For more details on the dataset and modeling work, visit the companion repository:
[Logistic Regression Predicting Student Dropout](https://github.com/adnan-mayof/Logistic-Regression-predicting-Student-Dropout)

---

## 📈 Insights from the Dashboard

Exploration highlights six variables strongly related to dropout:

* Application Mode
* Tuition Fees Up to Date
* Age at Enrollment
* Course
* Scholarship Holder
* Curricular Units (2nd Semester Grade)

By toggling the **Dropout filter**, you can observe how these variables differ between dropout and non-dropout groups.
